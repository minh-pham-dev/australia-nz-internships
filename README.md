# 🇦🇺🇳🇿 Australia & New Zealand Internships 2027/28

A free, community-run list of **summer internships, vacationer programs and graduate roles in Australia and New Zealand**, across tech, finance, consulting, engineering and more.

Every role shows the thing most job boards bury at the bottom of the ad: **can international students actually apply?**

> 🙌 Found a new role or a mistake? [Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) or read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
> ⭐ Star the repo so you can find it again when applications open.

## 📅 When to apply

ANZ employers hire **9–12 months ahead**. For a summer internship in **Nov 2027 – Feb 2028**:

| Sector | Usually opens |
|---|---|
| Banks, Big 4, consulting | Feb – Apr 2027 |
| Big tech & quant | Feb – Jun 2027 |
| Engineering & government | Mar – Aug 2027 |

## 🔑 Legend

| Symbol | Meaning |

| ❓ Unknown | The ad doesn't say. Ask the employer |

## ⚠️ Important

- Not affiliated with any employer or university.
- Rules change every year. **Always read the official ad before applying.**
- Nothing here is migration advice. Check your own visa conditions on [VEVO (Australia)](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/already-have-a-visa/check-visa-details-and-conditions) or with [Immigration New Zealand](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/).

## 🛠️ How it works

The tables are generated from [`data/listings.yml`](data/listings.yml):

```bash
pip install pyyaml
python3 scripts/generate_readme.py --check   # validate the data
python3 scripts/generate_readme.py           # rebuild the tables
```
